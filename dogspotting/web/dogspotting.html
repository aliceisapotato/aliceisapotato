    How to play

    1. Find a dog. Ask the owner before you take a photo.
    2. Tap the red ball to open your camera. Get the whole dog in frame: head, legs and tail.
    3. The scanner reads the dog's head, ears, coat, colours, size and tail, then names the breed.
    4. Each new breed goes into your Dogdex. Climb the ranks by finding rarer breeds.
